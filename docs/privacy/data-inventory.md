# Launch data inventory

This inventory must be updated when a feature or provider is implemented.

| Flow | Data categories | Data subject | Purpose | Preliminary legal basis | Sharing | Retention owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public shell | Request metadata and security logs | Visitor | Delivery and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest with minimization | Hostinger/Cloudflare | Thales Gomes |
| Account | Name, email, password hash, legal acceptance version/date | User | Create and secure an account | Contract steps/performance and security | Hostinger; Google backup only | Thales Gomes |
| Session | Session identifier, user identifier, timestamps, IP/security metadata | User | Authenticate and prevent abuse | Contract performance and legitimate interest | Hostinger/Cloudflare; Google backup | Thales Gomes |
| Workspace | Membership, role, invitations | User/invitee | Provide multi-tenant collaboration | Contract performance | Hostinger; Google backup | Workspace owner/Thales Gomes |
| Work content | Projects, tasks, comments, evidence, decisions, activity | People referenced by users | Provide the requested backlog | Controller role depends on who determines workspace use | Hostinger; Google backup | Workspace owner/Thales Gomes |
| Service account | Name, scopes, token prefix/hash, use timestamps | Technical principal; may relate to operator | Agent access and security audit | Contract performance and legitimate interest | Hostinger; Google backup | Workspace owner/Thales Gomes |
| Rights request | Identity verification, request, decision and response | Requester | Comply with LGPD rights | Legal obligation | Legal/support provider if selected | Privacy owner |

No payment, attachment, advertising, analytics, or model-training flow is approved in V1. A RIPD screening is required before processing sensitive data, vulnerable groups, large-scale profiling, or automated decisions with material impact.
